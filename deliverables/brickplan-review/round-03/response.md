# Response to final publication review

The independent publication review is archived byte-for-byte in this directory.
The accepted source, task bank, labels, geometry, observations, manuscript,
figures, PDFs and runtime archives are unchanged after that review.

The orchestration step preserves all three review snapshots, the revision
responses, final download and extracted-runtime evidence, and the Git index
comparison. The earlier workbench integration report remains unchanged:
its download-pending note records the time of that check. The later
`../download-validation.json` provides the completed final download checks.

A repository attribute outside the research snapshot preserves original
source-archive line endings on checkout. The original source files are not
reformatted to silence whitespace notices. Authored code/document checks pass;
the preserved CAD, template, generated JavaScript and CSV exceptions are
explicitly enumerated in `../git-whitespace-check.json`.

No new population model or human collection was performed. The verdict is an
internal simulated assessment under the protocol's experiment-completion
assumption, not an official CVPR decision or evidence of empirical superiority.

Git commit/push verification follows this pre-commit dossier. Its resulting
commit is recorded separately to avoid a self-referential commit hash.
