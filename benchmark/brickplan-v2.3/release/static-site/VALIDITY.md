The main taxonomy is task mechanism. D1–D4 are **optimal-operation-length bins**
(1–8, 9–16, 17–24, 25+) and do not measure necessary reasoning depth.

## Future-sequence controls

Interior-order pairs use an exact legal completion and a reordered
sequence with the same first action, last action and action multiset. Both
sequences have an identical legal prefix before the first conflict. No no-op
padding or repeated-state walks are used. The 2–5, 6–10 and 11+ conflict-position
bins are balanced by the declared diagnostic sampling rule.

A prefix-only control executes a declared number of operations and otherwise
predicts “all legal.” Results and full conflict-position counts are generated
from `data/upgrade-audit.json`. These are software shortcut audits, not
language-model results or proof of immunity to other shortcuts.

## Source sampling

Alternate builds and geometry variants stay within their kit-level source
group. New source identities, intake strata, hash-assigned source splits,
geometry rules, and seeded sampling were frozen before new geometry or solver
discovery. Exact development and held-out membership is in `source-plan.json`.
New sources do not imply absence from foundation-model pretraining.

New admission requires at least eight registered and eight independently
reachable movable sites. Deterministic endpoint pruning and bounded
stud-connected extraction preserve original instance identities and matrices.
No pieces are moved or invented. The predecessor's earlier geometry-pruning
refinement remains documented in frozen 2.2; its history is not retroactively
described as preregistered.

The independent original-hierarchy and source-identity audit is an additional
release gate after preliminary geometric admission. Five candidates (10124,
10226, 10261, 10262, 21318) are excluded because part-only manifests omit
direct model geometry; 30312 is excluded for a conflicting original root kit
identifier. Previous admissions are preserved in `data/source-admission-history/`.
No source bytes, author headers or candidate identities are repaired or replaced.
Every retained new-source index is checked against its original-reference leaf
proof, including exact instance ID, part/color and composed source matrix.

Each new source supplies two goals, three job settings (0/2/4 jobs), and
two starts per mission. Task starts, goals and work orders use seeded sampling;
admission depends on geometric qualification and solvability, not policy
performance, heuristic gap or expansion count. Easy cases are retained.
Geometry rejection reasons and the exact retained source indices are released.
All rejected candidates remain in the admission census, with source instances,
qualification attempts, and reasons. Historical small domains remain
regression/control cases, not new-source admissions. Source scale does not
remove geometric dependence or admission bias.

## Visual context and appearance controls

Primary answer-changing future-sequence pairs hold total part count, exact
part-number/color inventory, registered instances, source poses, initial
occupancy, goals, capacity, work orders and the proposed plan constant.
Only an induced subset of the original fixed support
context changes. Both contexts are independently requalified against collision
meshes and straight-motion paths. The first conflict occurs after a shared
legal prefix of at least two actions.

Geometry-only context selection groups one/two-instance deletions by exact
inventory and selects distinct support-mask pairs in seeded hash order.
Connected fixed structures and full-assembly support are required; no
unequal-inventory fallback is permitted. At most four interventions per base
are selected before answer-change search, with at most two starting questions
retained per intervention. Search-specific zero yield is not a proof that
no contrast exists. Candidate subsets, qualifying interventions, retained
starts, sources, and held-out coverage are separate reported units.

The earlier unequal-inventory contrasts remain
`historical-unmatched-regression`; they never enter `primary-matched` scores.
Additional appearance pairs compare original-color and
grayscale observations of the exact same state and plan. Answers must remain
unchanged. These are diagnostic controls, not independent source examples.

Static diagnostic inputs supply isometric, left, rear and right views at
revision zero. Active inputs start with isometric and charge later views.
The native certificate compares all five panels in each view and records
pair-specific differences. Equal global inventories do not imply identical
spatial evidence. Pixel distinction is not human/model answerability.

## Exact paired scoring and shortcuts

Primary success requires **both exact first-conflict labels**, not a binary
answer flip. A pair enters the denominator whenever either member is assigned.
A missing partner, absent response, malformed answer, or wrong conflict index
prevents joint credit. Wholly unassigned pairs remain census-only.
The hierarchy averages repeats within distinct starts, starts within
interventions, interventions within sources, then sources equally.
Bootstrap intervals resample whole sources. Fewer than two assigned sources
leaves the interval undefined; it is never replaced with a fake zero interval.
Leave-one-source-out summaries and assignment/missing counts accompany scores.

`data/matched-controls.json` reports count-only, type/color-presence,
type/color-count, sequence-only, and explicitly scoped metadata certificates.
These label-aware finite-bank information ceilings are not fitted held-out
predictions. Equal reduced inputs with unequal labels preclude deterministic
joint correctness for that representation, not for a richer policy or one
using pair position. Global presence is not instance-sensitive spatial presence.
Separate label-free rules execute inventory constraints without geometric
relations. Source majority fits only primary development labels and applies
its frozen pooled fallback to unseen held-out sources. Inventories and named
occupancy assume privileged extraction, not measured perception. Source
uncertainty is conditional on the retained finite bank, not a population claim.

## Delayed repair

Delayed-repair questions start after a certified legal but avoidably
costly action and a fixed lag of 1, 3 or 5 subsequent legal operations.
The executed history visits distinct states and remains outside the pre-error
optimal corridor. Repair means reaching a **nonterminal** state on any optimal
completion from the pre-error checkpoint. Reaching the goal alone does not
count. Exact checkpoint-specific minimum repair costs and cohort ranges
are generated from the final labels.

The evaluator separately tracks spontaneous errors, immediate return, and
delayed cohorts at lags 1/3/5. Earlier errors remain active when later errors
occur. Successful completion, conditional excess and optimal completion are
primary outcomes; immediate inverse-operation repair efficiency is secondary.

Optimal, suboptimal, and unavailable inverse-last cases are separate reporting
cohorts. All-correct-choice rows
remain calibration only and are excluded from discriminative accuracy.
Canonical identities cluster repeated probes into diagnostic problems.
Primary accuracy averages assigned variants within problems, then problems
within sources, then sources, retaining zero credit for missing responses.

## Qualification and unresolved empirical claims

`audit_v21.py` replays every retained contrast, checks identical prefixes and
action multisets, verifies source-group separation, and checks delayed repair
against an independent forward BFS. `validate.py` covers exact oracle
trajectories, all diagnostic answers, assignment denominators, state tampering,
recovery, action budgets and observation isolation. `audit_v23.py` binds every
active question to source manifests, original identities/matrices, and exact
matched inventories. Archived independent rules recompute costs and answers.
Publication generation must wait for matching bank hashes and complete audits;
a source-provenance check alone is not an all-answer correctness certificate.

The geometry contract is conservative stud connectivity plus prescribed
straight translations over guarded inset meshes. It is not a force simulator,
free-pose assembly or unrestricted robotics benchmark. Depth-one heuristic
tie accuracy uses privileged relations and is not visual performance.
Finite-horizon bounds do not establish internal reasoning depth.
Population VLM and human experiments remain pending.
