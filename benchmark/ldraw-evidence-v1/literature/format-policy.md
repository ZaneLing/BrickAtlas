# Manuscript format baseline

The verified baseline is the **current-year CVPR 2026 author guideline**
accessed 2026-09-19 and official author-kit tag `CVPR2026-v1(latex)`.
This records a formatting baseline, not an assertion that a submission
has been registered or that the conference remains open for submission.
An eventual different conference/year requires checking its own rules.

The guideline HTML, original template ZIP and hashes are preserved in
`format-download-manifest.json`. The template's `main.tex`, `preamble.tex`,
`cvpr.sty` and `ieeenat_fullname.bst` are extracted under `author-kit/`.

- Main text, figures and tables must fit within eight pages in the CVPR
  template. Additional pages may contain only cited references.
- Review manuscripts use the review option, line/page numbers and anonymous
  authors. Supplementary material must also avoid author-identifying content.
- The template uses 10-point, two-column US Letter text. Figures and captions
  must be legible at printed size; captions use normal numbered figure/table
  environments.
- Supplementary PDFs may use a legible single-column format; the guideline
  imposes no separate supplementary PDF formatting requirement.
- Contributed content must be self-contained in submitted files. An external
  project link cannot substitute for required evidence or bypass page limits.
  Citations of prior work are distinct from links that expand contributed
  content.

The revised manuscript will use an unchanged copy of the downloaded style,
`review` mode and a clearly nonregistered draft paper ID. Authors/affiliations
are not invented. Reproducibility artifacts and third-party source attribution
remain in the local release; private machine paths are excluded from PDFs.
Final verification checks PDF pages, references, figure captions, English
text, font embedding and content within page bounds. Visual page inspection
is recorded separately; successful compilation alone is not acceptance.
