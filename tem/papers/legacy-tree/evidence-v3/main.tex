\begin{abstract}
Answering a visual question correctly does not establish that a model
tracks the evidence selected by its reference. A paired test can address
this gap only if the reference and the answer remain well defined after
the intervention. We introduce a display-grounded observation contract
on \EvSources{} human-authored LDraw assemblies. Numeric labels select
objects in the current image; canonical source identities provide audit
provenance, not immutable answers to edited images. Two separate families
test rendered color changes at fixed geometry (\EvColor{} pairs) and
candidate-reference reassignment for geometry matching (\EvType{} pairs).
A finite assignment optimization removes a previously perfect option-order
shortcut and bounds 13 declared A-conditioned rules before inference.
Human assignment prevents any reviewer seeing two views of one parent.
A frozen three-model roster binds every planned run and failure to analysis.
A supporting graph audit demonstrates why the choice of control matters:
an isolates-plus-one heuristic solves 72/73 natural questions and all
61 degree-visible change pairs, but has zero both-correct on 12
degree-matched pairs; BFS solves all of them. The strong subset is a
small proof of concept, including an anonymous 1-WL blind spot.
We release machine-verified stimuli, the executable evaluation protocol,
and a searchable \EvTasks{}-item historical dossier. Human visual
certification and learned-model diagnostic value remain empirical
requirements; the reported findings concern stimulus and algorithmic
audits.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
A label may identify a source instance or select a visible object.
These meanings diverge under intervention: relabeling another part
B0035 does not change the original B0035's type. Changing gold without
specifying the referent risks rewarding interpretation shifts.

We ask a narrower, observable question: \emph{does a response correctly
follow the property or candidate reference selected in the current
display, when that observation changes under an explicit contract?}
The contract resolves numeric references within the supplied image.
The source identity is retained for construction, pairing and inspection.
The model is never asked to recover a historical source fact from a
deliberately edited display.

We study two atomic visual families separately
(Figure~\ref{fig:pairs}). In Color, the same original geometry and
pose are rendered with two opaque body colors, while the camera,
labels and material settings are fixed. The question explicitly concerns
the current rendered appearance. In Part-type, target geometry and
its label are fixed, while two candidate labels exchange their visible
referents. The correct candidate token changes; no canonical part
changes its type. These interventions support different interpretations,
so they never contribute to a pooled visual score.

Paired image-dependent answers have substantial precedent in
VQA~\cite{goyal2017vqa}, contrast sets~\cite{gardner2020contrast}, and
joint grounding tests~\cite{thrush2022winoground}. Our contribution is
not the invention of pairing or both-correct scoring. It is a versioned
measurement contract that connects a well-defined current referent,
condition-specific gold, independent review of both exact observations,
and receipt-derived response transitions in a reusable assembly resource.
This connection matters because correct gold in a CAD record does not
guarantee a uniquely visible answer.

The supporting graph audit gives a concrete example of how inadequate
controls can defeat the intended interpretation. A shallow rule that
counts isolated surviving nodes and adds one for any remaining nodes
answers 72/73 natural component questions correctly. It also solves both
sides of all 61 change pairs whose degree distributions differ.
On 12 pairs matched in complete degree sequence, the same rule has
zero paired both-correct, while component algorithms solve all pairs.
This establishes an interpretive distinction between the distributions.
It does not establish that a learned model uses the heuristic.

Pairing alone is insufficient. An earlier Part-type assignment always
chose the first candidate different from A's gold, permitting perfect
Both without reading B. This revision jointly balances choice transitions,
audits explicit shortcut predictors, and freezes model inclusion before
inference. Its contributions connect display semantics, bounded stimulus
design and complete evidence collection. Whether this protocol reveals
learned-model differences beyond ordinary accuracy remains empirical.

\section{Related Work and Measurement Scope}
\paragraph{Assembly benchmarks.}
BrickNet~\cite{kulits2026bricknet} represents placed brick instances and
connectors in graph-backed generative assembly programs.
LEGO-Puzzles~\cite{tang2025legopuzzles} evaluates spatial/sequential
reasoning in editable scenes with three-annotator validation in its
pinned v3 protocol. PhyBlock~\cite{ma2025phyblock} studies physical
planning with dependencies and simulation. Numeric IDs and reusable
scene assets are established machinery; our endpoint concerns two
elementary visual properties, not assembly planning.

\paragraph{Contrastive and consistent answers.}
Contrast sets probe local decision boundaries using small meaningful
input changes~\cite{gardner2020contrast}. VQA v2 pairs similar images
that support different answers to the same question, reducing the
utility of language-only shortcuts~\cite{goyal2017vqa}.
Cycle-consistency and VQA-Rephrasings address answer stability under
linguistic variation~\cite{shah2019cycle}. Required answer change and
required invariance are different tests: a constant predictor can be
stable without responding to evidence. BrickAtlas records which
property an intervention changes and which answer policy applies,
rather than treating every response change as either success or failure.

\paragraph{Reference grounding and construct validity.}
Numeric labels are narrower than referring-expression
comprehension~\cite{mao2016refexp} or Winoground's joint image--caption
matching~\cite{thrush2022winoground}. Small objects, resolution and
off-construct requirements can explain grounding
failures~\cite{diwan2022winoground}; independent review therefore judges
exact questions and pixels, including visual answer uniqueness.

\paragraph{Controlled visual and graph evidence.}
CLEVR~\cite{johnson2017clevr} and GQA~\cite{hudson2019gqa} illustrate
the value of explicit scene structure and question operations.
BrickAtlas uses source structure for provenance while keeping it out
of visual requests. A separate text-graph challenge tests component
counting. The expressivity analysis of neighborhood aggregation
against Weisfeiler--Lehman refinement~\cite{xu2019gnn} clarifies a
limit of that challenge: our six-cycle/two-triangles pairs are
indistinguishable to anonymous uniform-feature 1-WL. This is a
known representational limitation instantiated by the audit, not a
new impossibility result or an expectation that a simple GNN must
solve the pairs.

\section{Resource and Reference Contract}
\subsection{Preserved sources and separate roles}
The historical resource has \EvTasks{} questions on \EvSources{}
human-authored assemblies and \EvParts{} placed parts in the LDraw
format~\cite{ldraw}. Original files, source transforms, questions and
answers remain unchanged. The 140 visual parents define observations in
display-v3; the other 477 historical items remain accessible controls
and structural tasks. A historical task ID is a provenance key, not
a key for transferring a response into the new contract.

Visual questions receive image/text, structural questions explicit graphs.
Historical record-reading and visibility controls remain separate.
Shared IDs connect these artifacts without making each question a
perception-to-action chain.

Source bands D1--D4 describe assembly scale, not cognitive difficulty.
The supplement lists every source; the separate dossier lists all families.
The stable schema key \texttt{shape-match} displays as Part-type.

\subsection{Four meanings that must remain distinct}
For a source hash $h$ and instance ID $i$, canonical identity
$c=(h,i)$ identifies the original record. Source truth $T(c)$ stores
its type, color and transform. In observation $o$, a display mapping
$d_o(\ell)$ selects the visible referent of label $\ell$. The scored
answer $y_o$ is a function of the question and this observation,
not necessarily of $T(c)$ alone.

Every new visual question includes the following rule:
\emph{References select objects in the supplied image only. A label
such as B0001 is an image-local display reference, not a request to
retrieve a historical source instance. Renders may have edited
appearances or label assignments. Answer only from the current image.}
This rule is part of the hashed model-visible text on both sides.
The evaluator retains $c$, $T(c)$ and the display mapping outside
the request.

Color asks, ``What color is the object currently marked B0035 in
this image?'' Part-type asks, ``Which displayed candidate label
currently marks an object with the same visible part geometry as
the target marked B0035? Ignore color and pose.'' The complete
Part-type prompt also repeats that the current image, rather than
historical identities, determines the answer. Options have explicit
choice IDs. The response contract requires exactly one JSON object
containing a listed \texttt{choiceId}.

\subsection{What is physically preserved}
Source geometry and poses are immutable. Isolated or operand-only
views may omit supports; they are observations, not freestanding
configurations. Color edits and separate part panels propose no new
physical assembly.

The supplied connector graph covers recognized relations, not every
contact or stability condition. The historical inventory still contains
111 other flagged tasks and 573 intersection candidates requiring
human decisions. A candidate may be intended mating, an approximate
mesh intersection or a source defect. This revision does not resolve
those physical judgments through visual benchmark code.

\section{Interventions and Exact Observations}
\subsection{Color: controlled rendered appearance}
The \EvColor{} Color pairs use the same original geometry, source
pose, camera, scale, label projections, visibility and material
settings. Only the body-color parameter changes between two distinct
named opaque colors. Both arms explicitly permit edited appearance;
neither arm asks for the original source color. The 146 observations
use Red, Blue, Yellow and Black, with aggregate counts 37, 37, 36 and
36 respectively. These are displayed gold categories, not a sample
of natural material colors.

Color retains its display-v2 image/text bytes. It tests named rendered
appearance, not illumination invariance, material recognition or
recovery of natural object color.

The machine audit checks common camera and label metadata and compares
the two native images. All 73 pairs have zero changed pixels outside
the common projected body mask dilated by one antialiasing pixel.
Independent regeneration of three canary observations is byte-identical.
These checks locate the pixel change; they cannot establish that
humans perceive every chosen category unambiguously.

\subsection{Part-type: candidate-reference reassignment}
The \EvType{} Part-type pairs retain the target geometry and token.
The matching candidate exchanges labels with one other candidate,
so the current reference that selects matching geometry changes.
The option list remains fixed within the pair. Source part numbers
independently derive a candidate gold, but the question asks for visible
geometry. Similar types, hidden features or a confusing label leader
can defeat visual uniqueness even when the record-level match is exact.

Both Part-type arms receive new option orders, image renders, observation
IDs and review lineage. Base geometry pixels match exactly within each
pair; changed pixels lie within the label masks. Two regenerated
canaries are byte-identical. In Figure~\ref{fig:pairs}, B0035 stays fixed
while the matching candidate's label changes from B0036 to B0116.
No old Part-type review or response is transferable.

\subsection{Joint assignment and bounded shortcut audit}
For each parent we enumerate all four-option permutations and all
nonmatching swap candidates already visible in the operand set, with an
on-screen anchor and exactly one source-type match. The 4,824
configurations reduce to 2,412 constraint-equivalent representatives.
Binary selection chooses one configuration per parent. Bounds are
committed before optimization: each arm has 16 or 17 golds per position,
each off-diagonal transition has 5 or 6 pairs, and diagonal counts are zero.
Every fixed-choice predictor must score at most .30 source-macro on
either arm. Thirteen declared oracle-A rules must have B accuracy at
most .40 in both micro and source-macro estimates.

The objective minimizes summed integer SHA-256 configuration costs;
sorted inputs and the checked single-thread HiGHS runtime give the
recorded optimum 1,007,668. The immutable assignment is authoritative
across runtimes. Rules use option order, alternate label numbers or
nearest/farthest A-side source centers and projected anchors; none reads
B pixels. Independent gold/feature reconstruction enforces the contract
before sealing, live inference and publication. These are design bounds,
not significance thresholds or evidence against every possible shortcut.

Tables~\ref{tab:marginals} and \ref{tab:priors} expose actual priors.
The former perfect first-alternative attack now has 23/67 Both
(34.33\% micro; 26.94\% source). Because each transition cell is at most
6, any single deterministic mapping from A choice alone to B choice has
in-sample accuracy at most $4(6)/67=35.82\%$. This restricted ceiling
does not apply to predictors using labels, source IDs or additional
features. Color was not reoptimized: its last-alternative and cyclic
previous rules reach 32/73. Balanced semantic color totals therefore
do not imply balanced choice transitions or a shortcut-free family.

A separate set of 67 position-reference observations shows a target
and four candidate panels without text inside the image. Options
refer to candidate positions. This provides a geometry-matching
comparison under a different reference interface. Panel layout and
visual access also change; a difference from label-based performance
cannot by itself identify an OCR or label-tracking effect. The control
has its own human reviews and never enters primary paired Both.

\subsection{Freezing the input boundary}
An exact observation includes the complete system/user messages,
question, options, native 1280$\times$800 PNG bytes, resolution and
image-detail policy. All 347 observations are frozen before review:
280 primary endpoints and 67 auxiliary controls. Rendering metadata,
canonical source truth, conditions, pairing and gold are evaluator-only
records. Neither endpoint is supplied as conversational history to the
other.

The reviewed observation is distinct from the provider transport
envelope. A run pins its model revision, endpoint adapter and generation
settings, and separately hashes the full HTTP request. The runner must
reproduce the reviewed messages and image bytes inside that envelope.
This supports comparisons across pinned models without asserting
that a provider's internal image preprocessing is observable. Any
change to reviewed text, pixels, dimensions or image policy requires
new review; changing only the pinned provider envelope does not create
a new visual question.

\section{Human Qualification and Response Analysis}
\subsection{Independent review of both sides}
Six distinct reviewers receive 115, 116, 114, 115, 115 and 119 units,
respectively, in shuffled queues with opaque unit IDs. Every observation
receives two judgments, but nobody sees more than one observation of
a parent, including the auxiliary panel. Thus A/B reviewer sets have
empty intersection; reviewer slots do not identify an arm. The interface
shows one observation at a time, its exact text and scrollable native
pixels. It withholds the condition name, parent/pair membership,
source record, gold, model output and the other reviewer's decision.
The primary denominator is always 280; it cannot become a B-only
queue through task-ID deduplication.

A raw review contains the reviewer pseudonym, timestamp, categorical
decision, visual choice, reason, and attestations of native-size
inspection and independence. Four structured checks ask whether
the target reference is unique, exactly one option is visually correct,
visibility is sufficient, and the observation lacks reference
contradictions. Decisions distinguish decidable, ambiguous,
unanswerable and contradictory cases.

Matching decisions, choices and checks produce a final judgment.
Any disagreement among these fields requires explicit adjudication.
Up to three additional people occupy adjudicator slots; none is an
initial reviewer or sees multiple observations of a parent. Assignment,
imports and final reconstruction all verify this exposure rule.
Both original records and raw adjudication exports remain intact.
A final decidable choice that conflicts with the generated gold blocks
finalization and requires revising the item and repeating QA, rather
than silently replacing the answer. Agreement reports include raw
decision agreement, multiclass Cohen's kappa, choice/check agreement
and their actual denominators. Undefined kappa remains undefined.

The runner requires final decisions for every planned endpoint before
real visual inference. Nondecidable final judgments remain in the full
denominator; only pairs with two final decidable endpoints enter the
additional QA subset. This prevents post-result item selection.
The gate recomputes decisions from retained raw files. It verifies
recorded provenance and attestations, not a person's identity through
cryptography. Coordinator oversight remains necessary.

\subsection{Correct adaptation and failure denominators}
For pair $i$, let $p_{Ai},p_{Bi}$ be normalized choices, $y_{Ai}\ne
y_{Bi}$ their golds, and $v_{Ai},v_{Bi}$ indicate valid output.
Invalid or missing predictions are never equal to a gold. For
$N$ fixed pairs, we report
\begin{align}
A\mathrm{Acc}&=N^{-1}\sum_i[p_{Ai}=y_{Ai}],\\
\mathrm{NewAcc}&=N^{-1}\sum_i[p_{Bi}=y_{Bi}],\\
\mathrm{Both}&=N^{-1}\sum_i[p_{Ai}=y_{Ai}][p_{Bi}=y_{Bi}].
\end{align}
The B outcomes partition into new-gold correctness, old-gold retention,
another valid choice and invalid output. In particular,
\begin{equation}
R_{\rm old}=N^{-1}\sum_i v_{Bi}[p_{Bi}=y_{Ai}].
\end{equation}
Old-gold retention is a behavioral outcome, not proof of memory or an
internal causal mechanism.

Conditional adaptation is Both divided by A accuracy for the micro
estimate. Its denominator is the number of A-correct pairs; a zero
denominator yields null. Conditional source-macro analysis averages
within-source conditional rates only over sources with eligible pairs
and reports that coverage. We retain unconditional estimates so
conditioning cannot hide a weak A endpoint.

Format-valid rates are reported for A, B and both endpoints, alongside
semantic rates. Timeouts, API failures, refusals, missing receipts and
illegal formats remain incorrect in the planned denominator, with
separate failure reasons. A stable invalid string is not semantic
consistency. The auxiliary position control reports its own accuracy
and validity without being pooled with either visual family.

\subsection{Aggregation, selection and comparisons}
For a pair outcome $z_{si}$ from source $s$, the primary estimator is
\begin{equation}
\widehat\mu=\frac{1}{|S|}\sum_{s\in S}
                 \frac{1}{n_s}\sum_{i=1}^{n_s}z_{si}.
\end{equation}
Each family has one primary pair per parent. We also report micro
rates, pair counts and source counts. A family absent from a source
does not contribute an artificial zero. Percentile intervals use
10,000 whole-source bootstrap draws with seed 20260920. Resampling
sources preserves within-source dependence. We also freeze 18 primary
author groups and 17 contributor-connected groups from the source credits.
Sensitivity reports include equal-author means, author-cluster intervals
for both weighting schemes, and leave-one-author-out differences.
Contributor clustering also includes OMR editors. All 276 source-pair
part-inventory Jaccards are published; inventory overlap is not aligned
geometry. A direction changing under author omission limits any
cross-source stability claim.

Full and both-decidable estimates are published together with exclusion
reasons and source coverage. The QA-minus-full difference resamples
the same source weights for both estimates, recomputing each mean
on its eligible sources. This retains changes in source composition.
Overlapping exclusion reasons are not added as independent counts.
Empty subsets and bootstrap draws without selected sources are
explicitly recorded.

Model comparisons use paired outcomes on the same items. Ordinary-A,
NewAcc and Both ranks use exact rational point comparisons and
descending midranks for ties. A reversal means the signs of the A
and Both differences disagree; a tie is separately classified.
Paired source intervals accompany the differences. We also record
whether comparison directions agree between full and QA subsets.
The intervals are descriptive and unadjusted for multiple comparisons.
An interval containing zero does not support a directional ranking.
The estimand is one frozen invocation set, not cross-call stability;
temperature zero does not establish provider determinism.

\subsection{Natural evidence and hypothetical partitions}
A historical Graph deletion question asks for the number of connected
components after deleting a specified node from an explicit undirected
graph, including isolated survivors. Duplicate edges are deduplicated.
Independent BFS and union-find implementations agree on all golds.
These algorithms validate the supplied graph contract, not the
completeness of the physical connector detector.

The audit retains all 73 natural questions from 24 sources. It also
reclassifies the existing 219 hypothetical observations: each parent
has an anchor, an answer-changing partner and an isomorphic
answer-preserving partner. The 12 strong change pairs span eight
sources and match surviving node set, edge count, target degree,
complete surviving degree multiset and isolate count. The other
61 change pairs span 23 sources and are explicitly called
\emph{degree-visible controls}. Their invariance pairs are reported
separately. The shared anchors mean these four partitions cannot
be summed as independent observations.

Strong pairs compare a six-cycle with two triangles, with identical
extra isolated nodes. Weak pairs use a path versus a triangle plus
isolates. Both templates can match node and edge counts, but only the
former matches degrees. These are hypothetical edge lists derived
for a controlled test; they are not claims about newly discovered
physical connections in the source assemblies.

\subsection{Baselines and matching limits}
We include node-count, edge-count, degree-histogram and anonymous
1-WL feature lookups; the isolates-plus-one rule; and BFS/union-find.
For a surviving graph $G'$, the heuristic is
\begin{equation}
\widehat c_{\rm iso}=n_0+\mathbf{1}[|V(G')|>n_0],
\end{equation}
where $n_0$ is its isolate count. It does not traverse components.
Feature lookups use labels only from other sources, select the most
frequent answer, break ties toward the smaller integer, and abstain
on unseen features. Abstentions count as incorrect. Training uses
changed endpoints once; invariance copies do not duplicate training
labels. Natural and hypothetical fits remain separate.

Anonymous uniform-feature 1-WL gives identical signatures on all strong
change pairs. A deterministic predictor restricted to that signature
must give the same answer to different golds, hence Both=0.
This also limits message passing no more expressive than
1-WL~\cite{xu2019gnn}. Unique IDs, positions, spectral and higher-order
methods are outside the claim; no trained GNN result is implied.

\subsection{Measured results and uncertainty}
Table~\ref{tab:graph} shows that isolates-plus-one obtains 72/73 natural
correct answers, compared with 73/73 for BFS. Its natural source-macro
accuracy is 97.92\%, with a descriptive 95\% source interval
[93.75, 100]. Degree-histogram and 1-WL lookups reach 69/73.
Natural accuracy barely distinguishes traversal from these shallow rules.

On degree-visible change pairs, degree-histogram lookup, 1-WL lookup
and isolates-plus-one all achieve 61/61 Both. On strong change pairs,
each obtains 0/12; BFS and union-find obtain 12/12.
Strong invariance illustrates another distinction: the degree and
1-WL predictors are consistent across the two isomorphic observations,
but only 6/12 pairs are jointly correct. Invariance of predictions
alone would reward the six stable wrong pairs.

Strong change directions have six increases and six decreases, although
raw arm histograms differ. Unordered Both and symmetric endpoint
accuracy retain the 12-pair denominator. Histograms, all strong pairs
and invariance results appear in the supplement.

Approximate pair Wilson intervals are [0, .2425] for 0/12 and
[.7575, 1] for 12/12; pair independence is imperfect.
Whole-source intervals can degenerate at boundary outcomes.
Neither establishes generality beyond eight sources and one template.

\section{Reproducibility, Limits and Empirical Acceptance}
Earlier release bytes and source render assets are archived and hashed.
New Part-type IDs prohibit transferring earlier reviews/responses.
Empirical analysis reparses raw outputs, validates served revisions
and checks the full roster, gold provenance and common QA snapshot.
Dry runs and fixtures cannot enter empirical reporting.

Machine verification covers 347 frozen visual observations, independently
derived gold and shortcut rules, label/body pixel audits, all 2,739 planned
request envelopes and 27 new protocol/analysis tests. The 26 historical
tests remain preserved. These checks establish implementation behavior,
not real human agreement or remote-provider authenticity. Collection
oversight and retained provider records must support receipt provenance.

The release has zero genuine human reviews and 0/9 completed model runs.
The primary empirical question remains unresolved. Actual judgments,
full/QA results, retention, format effects and genuine cases are required.

The supplement gives protocol details, source coverage and all strong
pairs. The separate 617-item dossier links to a 3D viewer with rotation,
zoom, expansion and action replay. Those inspection views do not
substitute for native-input human qualification.

The bounded scope is instance-label-grounded evidence following on
two atomic visual attributes and 24 sources. Rendered colors do not
cover natural color constancy; candidate-label changes do not establish
general referring-expression understanding. The auxiliary panel
comparison changes visual access. Graph evidence concerns a limited
recognized graph and a small hypothetical challenge. Public source
reuse also leaves contamination and cross-source dependence concerns.
These limits bound what future positive results could establish.
