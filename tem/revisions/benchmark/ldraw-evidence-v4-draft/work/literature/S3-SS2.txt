3.2 Evaluation Metrics We report two pair-level metrics. Pair accuracy, Acc p \text{Acc}_{\text{p}} , is the fraction of pairs for which the model answers both images correctly. It is stricter than per-image accuracy because solving one side of a pair does not solve the paired flip. Collapse Rate (CR) measures update failure among pairs where the model shows at least partial task competence. For a pair ( v o , v e ) (v_{o},v_{e}) with fixed question q q and flipped gold answers y o ≠ y e y_{o}\neq y_{e} , let y ^ o \hat{y}_{o} and y ^ e \hat{y}_{e} be the model predictions, and let c o , c e c_{o},c_{e} indicate correctness on the two sides. A pair is competent if c o + c e ≥ 1 c_{o}+c_{e}\geq 1 ; among competent pairs, it collapses when the model gives the same non-empty answer to both images. We define: CR = | { i : c o i + c e i ≥ 1 ∧ y ^ o i = y ^ e i ≠ ∅ } | | { i : c o i + c e i ≥ 1 } | . \mathrm{CR}=\frac{\bigl|\{i:c_{o}^{i}+c_{e}^{i}\geq 1\;\wedge\;\hat{y}_{o}^{i}=\hat{y}_{e}^{i}\neq\emptyset\}\bigr|}{\bigl|\{i:c_{o}^{i}+c_{e}^{i}\geq 1\}\bigr|}. (1) A high CR indicates that the model keeps a single answer across the visual perturbation even though the gold answer changed. Because each VisualFLIP pair is constructed with a deterministic answer flip, the “original”/“edited” designation is arbitrary for this metric; CR is invariant to exchanging the two labels, since it conditions only on competence (correct on either side) rather than on the original side specifically. Together, Acc p \text{Acc}_{\text{p}} and CR separate paired correctness from answer updating: Acc p \text{Acc}_{\text{p}} asks whether both images are solved, while CR asks whether a competent model changes its final response when the gold answer flips. We report cross-category and task-type breakdowns in Section 4 ; appendix analyses cover fine-grained task and scope details.
