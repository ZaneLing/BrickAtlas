4.1 Setup Models. We evaluate a broad suite of MLLMs, split by access and inference protocol. 1 1 1 For each model we cite the vendor’s model card or technical report for the closest available version; specific point-release cards may not be public at the time of writing. The open-source group contains standard VLMs such as GLM-4.6V ( GLM-V Team, 2025 ) , Qwen3-VL (235B, 32B, 8B) ( Qwen Team, 2025 ) , Qwen2.5-VL-7B ( Bai et al., 2025 ) , MiMo-v2.5 ( Xiaomi LLM-Core Team, 2025 ) , and Kimi K2.6 ( Kimi Team, 2025 ) . The open-source, tool-augmented group contains PixelReasoner-8B ( Su et al., 2025 ) , DeepEyes-7B ( Zheng et al., 2025 ) , DeepEyesV2-7B ( Hong et al., 2025 ) , Mini-o3-7B ( Lai et al., 2025 ) , and CoF-7B ( Zhang et al., 2025 ) ; all five are reinforcement-learning fine-tunes of Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct , an instruction-tuned model in the Qwen-VL series ( Bai et al., 2023 ; Wang et al., 2024b ) , which offers a controlled comparison around a shared backbone. The closed-source group includes GPT-4o ( Hurst et al., 2024 ) , GPT-5-mini ( Singh et al., 2026 ) and GPT-5.5 ( OpenAI, 2026 ) , Claude Opus 4.6 ( Anthropic, 2026a ) and Claude Opus 4.7 ( Anthropic, 2026b ) , Gemini 3.1 Pro ( Gemini Team, Google DeepMind, 2026a ) and Gemini 3.5 Flash ( Gemini Team, Google DeepMind, 2026b ) , Grok 4.3 ( xAI, 2026 ) , Qwen3.5-Flash and Qwen3.6-Plus ( Qwen Team, 2025 ) , GLM-5V-Turbo ( GLM-V Team, 2025 ) , and Seed 2.0 Mini ( Guo et al., 2025 ) . Decoding follows a single answer-first protocol with a 4096 4096 -token budget; full details are in Appendix A.4 . Benchmark. VisualFLIP contains 687 image pairs (1,374 images). It includes 515 synthetic pairs from 13 task templates and 172 real-image pairs derived from MathVision. All pairs follow the same answer-flip contract and use four categories: Cardinality, Attribute, Spatial, and Logic. For finer analysis, the synthetic templates are grouped into nine task types in Figures 2 and 6 .
